Talks & Presentations

  1. Oct 2026

    UEG Week 2026: Young Co-Chair and three abstract presentations

    UEG Week 2026 · Barcelona, Spain

    Young Co-Chair of the moderated poster session “Therapeutics in IBD: Real-world experience (I)”, plus three accepted abstracts on NLP/LLM-based Montreal classification, transient elastography in heart failure, and a clinical case of clear cell sarcoma of the gastrointestinal tract.

  2. May 2026

    NLP-based classification of bowel preparation quality in colonoscopy reports

    Digestive Disease Week (DDW) 2026 · Chicago, IL, United States

    Poster Mo2176: automated bowel-preparation quality classification over 11,374 colonoscopy reports (TF-IDF + logistic regression, ~92% accuracy), selected for the AGA Innovation Themed Walking Tour.

  3. Oct 2025

    NLP-based classification of bowel preparation quality and its association with adenoma detection rate

    UEG Week 2025 · Berlin, Germany

    Late-breaking oral presentation: NLP classification of bowel-preparation quality (TF-IDF + logistic regression, 92% CV accuracy, 11,374 reports) and its association with adenoma detection rate (n = 5,420; ADR 20.4%).
